PROJECT 05 - INCIDENT FINDINGS
Environment: Arch Linux

1. Intel graphics (i915)
- Unsupported parameter: fastboot=1
- Removed from /etc/modprobe.d/i915.conf
- Regenerated initramfs and UKI with mkinitcpio -P
- After reboot: unknown parameter warning disappeared
- Remaining intel_bios_init warning
- Driver initialized and graphical.target active
- No visible graphics malfunction reported

2. Intel Wi-Fi (iwlwifi)
- Unsupported parameter: d0i3_disable=0
- Removed from /etc/modprobe.d/iwlwifi.conf
- Preserved power_save=1 and uapsd_disable=0
- After reboot: unsupported parameter warning disappeared
- Firmware loaded and NetworkManager reported Wi-Fi connected

3. Wireless regulatory database
- Initial error: regulatory.db not found (ENOENT)
- wireless-regdb package was initially absent
- Installed wireless-regdb from Arch Linux repositories
- Confirmed /usr/lib/firmware/regulatory.db exists
- After reboot: regulatory.db error no longer appeared

4. SMBus
- Seven "SMBus is busy" messages
- All occurred within approximately 5.6 milliseconds during startup
- i801_smbus driver bound to controller
- Root cause not established

5. ACPI
- Missing WIST symbol
- CNVW.IFUN and CNVW._DSM methods aborted
- Wi-Fi subsequently initialized and connected
- Root cause not established

6. Other observations
- iwlwifi Unhandled alg: 0x707 appeared twice
- No confirmed functional impact
- TLP warning indicated competing power-management configuration
- UFW BLOCK messages were not treated as proof of an attack

Assessment:
Three configuration/package problems were corrected and verified.
Remaining hardware-specific warnings are documented as unresolved.
